# Welcome to the TVET Swap Blog

We are excited to launch our new blog section! Here we will share updates, success stories, and tips for TVET trainers looking for mutual transfers.

## What is TVET Swap?

TVET Swap is the official platform for connecting TVET trainers across Kenya. Our goal is to make the mutual transfer process seamless and secure.

### Key Features

- **Secure Matches**: Only verified trainers can join.
- **Easy Search**: Find matches by county and course.
- **Direct Connection**: Connect with colleagues directly.
