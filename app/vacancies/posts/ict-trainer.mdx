# Job Description

We are looking for a qualified and motivated ICT Technical Trainer to join our team. The successful candidate will be responsible for training students in various ICT units and ensuring they acquire the necessary skills for the job market.

## Key Responsibilities

- Prepare and deliver lectures and practical sessions.
- Assess and evaluate student performance.
- Develop and update curriculum materials.
- Mentor and guide students in their academic and professional development.
- Participate in departmental meetings and activities.

## Requirements

- Must be registered with TVETA.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
- Proficiency in programming languages (Java, Python, C++).
- Knowledge of networking and system administration.

## How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit their applications, including a CV and cover letter, to the Principal by the deadline.
